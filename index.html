Known seasonal sale windows (reference only — not brand-specific predictions)

No tool can reliably predict when a specific small brand will run a sale — there's no public data source for that. What's reliably known is the big shared shopping calendar, so treat these as "watch closely" windows rather than guarantees any one item will be discounted:

  • Black Friday — day after Thanksgiving (Nov 27, 2026)
  • Cyber Monday — the following Monday (Nov 30, 2026)
  • Green Monday — second Monday of December, a common second wave of online discounts
  • Christmas week clearance — Dec 24–31, often the deepest markdowns of the year on apparel
  • New Year / January clearance — early Jan, end-of-season clearing for winter stock
